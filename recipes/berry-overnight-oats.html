Berry Overnight Oats

Healthy, nutritious, and so lightweight

Creamy, fruity berry overnight oats that chill in the fridge so you have a fresh, grab-and-go breakfast waiting for you in the morning.

Check how simple this recipe is 😋

  • ½ cup quick or old fashioned oats, (such as Quaker Oats), uncooked
  • ½ cup nonfat milk
  • 1 teaspoon chia seeds (Optional)
  • 1 cup fresh mixed berries and fruit
